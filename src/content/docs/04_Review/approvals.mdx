You can use approval states to categorize and show the current status of assets. 

#### Currently the approval states are:

#### - <Badge text="Approved" variant="success" />
#### - <Badge text="Rejected" variant="danger" />
#### - <Badge text="Review" variant="caution" />
#### - <Badge text="Draft" variant="tip" />


This can happen in multiple places
- inside the asset overview panel
- during the asset review process
- for multiple assets at the same time in the project overview panel
