## June 2026 <Badge text="Latest" variant="success" />

#### Unreal Engine Plugin <Badge text="Beta" variant="tip" />

- The Shale plugin for Unreal Engine is now available in beta. Install it and give us your feedback on Discord.
- Please see the [Unreal Engine integration page](/05_integrations/unreal-engine/) for information on installing the plugin.



#### Video Support <Badge text="Beta" variant="tip" />
- Basic video support has been added as a reviewable assets
- Currently limited to H264 files with a maximum size of 2 GB
